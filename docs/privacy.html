Privacy Policy

For the fa/thm Android application. Last updated 20 August 2026.

fa/thm is a personal finance application. It connects to your bank accounts, with your permission, to show you your own transactions and balances, and lets you ask questions about them in plain language. This policy describes exactly what data the app handles, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.

This policy covers the fa/thm mobile application and its supporting backend services. It does not cover any third-party service you reach from within the app, each of which has its own policy — those are linked below.

What we collect

CategoryWhat, specificallyWhy
Account identifiers Your email address and a generated user ID. To create your account, sign you in, and scope your data to you.
Financial account information The names, types, masked account numbers, and current balances of the bank accounts you choose to connect. To display your accounts and balances.
Transaction information Transaction date, amount, merchant or description, and category, for the accounts you connect. To display your spending and answer your questions about it.
Bank account and routing numbers Where your bank makes them available through Plaid. To identify accounts reliably across syncs.
Questions you ask The text of questions you type into the app's Ask screen. To generate an answer.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, photos, device identifiers for advertising, or your browsing activity. It contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

How your bank connection works

Bank connections are handled by Plaid Inc. When you link an account, you enter your bank credentials directly into Plaid's interface. Those credentials are never seen by, transmitted through, or stored by this application. Plaid returns an access token that lets us retrieve your account and transaction data; that token is stored on our server and never sent to your phone.

You can disconnect a bank at any time from within the app. Disconnecting revokes our access at Plaid and deletes the stored connection along with the account records attached to it.

How the Ask feature works

When you ask a question, the question text is sent to Anthropic's Claude API to be interpreted, along with a description of the shape of your ledger: the list of category names in use, the display labels of your connected accounts, and the earliest and latest dates you have data for.

Your individual transactions are not sent to Anthropic. The model receives that structural summary and your question, and returns a query which is then run against your own data on our server. Anthropic does not use data submitted through its API to train its models.

Where your data is stored

Application data is stored in a Postgres database hosted by Supabase, encrypted at rest and in transit. Access is enforced at the database level by row-level security policies, so each account can read only its own rows.

Authentication is handled by Supabase Auth. If you sign in with Google, Google provides us with your email address and a user identifier; we do not receive your Google password.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising. Data is shared only with the service providers needed to operate the app:

  • Plaid — bank connectivity and transaction retrieval.
  • Supabase — database, authentication, and server hosting.
  • Anthropic — natural-language interpretation of your questions, on the limited basis described above.
  • Google — only if you choose Google as your sign-in method.

We may also disclose data where required by law.

How long we keep it

Your transaction and account data is retained for as long as your account exists. Disconnecting a bank deletes that bank's connection and account records immediately. Deleting your account deletes everything, as described below.

Deleting your data

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. See the data deletion page for how, what gets deleted, and how long it takes.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Bank access tokens and API keys are held server-side only and are never included in the mobile application. Database access is scoped per-user by row-level security.

No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted over the internet.

Children

fa/thm is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.
