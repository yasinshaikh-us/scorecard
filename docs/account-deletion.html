Delete Your Data

For the fa/thm Android application (package com.fathom.app). Last updated 20 August 2026.

You can have your fa/thm account and all data associated with it deleted, at any time, for any reason. You do not need to have the app installed to make the request.

How to request deletion

Email yasin.shaikh@gmail.com with the subject line "fa/thm account deletion request", sent from the email address associated with your account.

Sending from the account's own email address is how the request is verified. If you can no longer send from that address, say so in the message and you will be asked for another way to confirm the account is yours — no data is deleted without that confirmation, because deletion is irreversible.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your bank connections Access is revoked at Plaid, so no further data can be retrieved from your bank.
Account and balance records Deleted permanently.
Transaction history and categories Deleted permanently.
Bank account and routing numbers Deleted permanently.
Your login account and email address Deleted permanently.

Nothing is retained after deletion except where we are legally required to keep it. There is no partial deletion and no archived copy kept for your later use — once the request is processed, the data is gone and cannot be restored.

How long it takes

Requests are processed within 30 days of verification, and usually much sooner. You will receive a confirmation once the deletion is complete.

Deleting just one bank connection

If you want to disconnect a single bank rather than delete your whole account, you can do that yourself from within the app, without contacting anyone. That revokes access at Plaid and removes the account records for that bank, and leaves the rest of your account intact.
